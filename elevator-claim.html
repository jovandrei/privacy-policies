Elevator File — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Elevator File ("the app") is a personal reference and checklist companion. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. Everything you enter stays in on-device storage only; the app requires no account, performs no analytics or tracking, and contains no advertising.

Permissions

The app may request notifications to remind you of items you chose to track. No data leaves your device.

Third parties

The app shares nothing with third parties and uses no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
