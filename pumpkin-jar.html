Pumpkin Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Pumpkin Jar ("the app") is an offline candy-estimation report viewer. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It requires no account, performs no analytics or tracking, and contains no advertising.

Permissions

The app requests storage/media access solely to display report images bundled inside the app itself. No files leave your device.

Third parties

The app shares nothing with third parties and uses no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app is suitable for all audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
